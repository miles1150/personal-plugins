                  Security & recovery
                  
  
                  Browser sign-in, assistant connections and your local receiver (bridge) are separate. Signing in does not install a receiver. Signing out ends only this browser session.
                  
  
                  Claim or unlink an existing Space
                  Both a recent account sign-in and the existing owner key are required. Claiming keeps existing connections and the owner key. Unlinking revokes account-paired device connections; legacy connections remain.
                  Emergency detach uses the owner key without account sign-in. Keep your recovery key private.
