# Waypost provenance

The service and bridge now live in this repository at `services/waypost`. The original Waypost repository remains available for full history. The service was imported from `miles1150/waypost@11c4668f63ff0ef2c67026ff12c061d6c7ac980f`; no production resources or data were moved by the import.

This skill and its references derive from Waypost commit `9f70cbf3779211544739ff7998b93895b8618944` under Apache-2.0 (see `UPSTREAM-LICENSE`). The current bridge includes monorepo OAuth and receiver reliability changes. Its pinned source tree is `763e6d567f192cf073940d6c37eae049b3f9a578`; it must not be represented as the unchanged upstream release. Marketplace CI builds that committed tree, records the actual monorepo commit and executable digest, and verifies them through the bundled launcher. The source plugin contains no binary; the platform distribution includes verified binaries. Historical upstream provenance remains readable for attribution, not as the identity of newly built bytes.
